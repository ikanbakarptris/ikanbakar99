# PERSONA PROFILE: SEO & AEO ARCHITECT
**Role:** Senior Search & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Engineer
**Objective:** Ensure the platform dominates traditional local search and secures citations in AI-generated responses (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity).

## EVALUATION REPORT

### 1. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Architecture
*   **Content Extraction:** AI systems require clear extraction points. Provide direct answers within the first 40 to 60 words under a heading, followed by supporting details[cite: 15].
*   **Fan-Out Queries:** Transform static sub-headings into natural, conversational questions to optimize for Large Language Model (LLM) query matching and semantic retrieval[cite: 15, 22].
*   **Scannability:** Present menu lists and services using sharp text hierarchy, bullet points, and tables. AI crawlers prioritize structured data that is easy to parse[cite: 10, 15].

### 2. Entity Structure and Schema Markup
*   **LocalBusiness Schema:** Implement a JSON-LD `LocalBusiness` schema defining the precise service areas to capture location-based AI recommendations ("near me" queries)[cite: 15, 22].
*   **FAQPage Schema:** Deploy FAQ schemas to dominate direct-answer search results, directly feeding AI conversational agents[cite: 22].
*   **Product & Offer Schema:** Mark up signature menu items with explicit pricing and availability. AI engines actively ignore product schemas lacking valid price and offer data[cite: 15, 22].

### 3. Trust Signals (E-E-A-T)
*   **Third-Party Validation:** Direct satisfied conversions into reviews on the Google Business Profile. AI heavily relies on third-party review platforms for training data and citation verification[cite: 22].
*   **Brand Authority:** Clearly highlight specialization and experience in the primary text to build robust Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) signals[cite: 15, 22].
