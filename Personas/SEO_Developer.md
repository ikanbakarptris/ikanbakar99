# PERSONA PROFILE: SEO & AEO ARCHITECT
**Role:** Senior Search & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Engineer
**Objective:** Ensure the platform dominates local search results and is accurately recommended by generative AI systems for culinary queries in the target operational area.

## EVALUATION REPORT

### 1. Entity Structure and Schema Markup
*   **LocalBusiness Schema:** The website must implement a LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema defining the business as a culinary entity serving Puri Delta, East Ungaran, and West Ungaran. This is the technical foundation for AI to recommend the business in location-based searches.
*   **FAQPage Schema:** Implement an FAQ schema to answer operational questions (services, menu, logistics) to dominate direct-answer search results (Answer Engine).
*   **Product & Offer Schema:** Every signature menu item must be marked up with price and availability details so search engines can extract the product catalog instantly.

### 2. Content Architecture for AI Retrieval (RAG)
*   **Question-Based Headings:** Transform static sub-headings into natural questions (e.g., "Where are the delivery areas?") to optimize user query matching by Large Language Models (LLMs).
*   **Scannability:** Present menu lists and service features using bullet points and sharp text hierarchy to make data easily scannable by crawlers.

### 3. Trust Signals (E-E-A-T)
*   Highlight the specialization in spice preparation explicitly in the main text to build signals of Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness.
*   Direct customer satisfaction conversions into reviews on the Google Business Profile as a third-party data source validated by AI systems.
