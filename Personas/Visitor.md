# PERSONA PROFILE: UNDECIDED CUSTOMER
**Role:** Target market local resident (Puri Delta & Surrounding Areas)
**Objective:** Seeking a practical, fast, and convincing food ordering solution without having to go through a long and confusing checkout process.

## EVALUATION REPORT

### 1. Instant Clarity & Trust Signals
*   **Clarity Above the Fold:** Desires an interface that immediately presents "Menu Boom" and "Special Menu" options without forcing deep scrolling.
*   **Area Validation:** Needs explicit confirmation of service areas on the main page to alleviate doubts regarding delivery distance and eligibility.

### 2. Cognitive Load
*   **Progressive Profiling:** A multi-step form approach that breaks the process into 3 short stages is highly effective at reducing confusion, leveraging the sunk-cost bias, and accelerating the order completion rate.
*   **Visual Scanning:** The use of category badges helps speed up the selection process when the user has a short attention span.

### 3. Personal Interaction Dynamics
*   Turning market research data collection steps into order preference questions creates a perception of personalized service, instantly elevating the product's value in the eyes of the consumer.
