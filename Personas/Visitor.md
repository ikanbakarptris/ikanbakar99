# PERSONA PROFILE: UNDECIDED CUSTOMER
**Role:** Target market local resident
**Objective:** Seeking a practical, fast, and secure ordering solution with minimal friction and immediate clarity.

## EVALUATION REPORT

### 1. 50-Millisecond Trust Validation
*   **First Impression:** Users form trust judgments regarding a website's credibility in just 50 milliseconds[cite: 31]. The interface must immediately present core offerings above the fold without forcing deep scrolling[cite: 227].
*   **Point-of-Hesitation Signals:** Place critical trust signals (e.g., explicit delivery area confirmation, secure checkout guarantees) directly next to the Call-to-Action buttons to alleviate doubts exactly when they arise[cite: 12, 28, 31].

### 2. Frictionless Progressive Profiling
*   **Cognitive Load Reduction:** A 9-field form drops conversions to 3.6%, while a 3-field form converts at 10.1%[cite: 256]. Utilize a multi-step form (Progressive Profiling) to break the order process into 2-3 highly focused stages[cite: 229, 238].
*   **Sunk-Cost Momentum:** Capturing low-friction data first (like selecting a menu item) leverages the sunk-cost effect, significantly increasing the likelihood that the user will complete the checkout details on the final step[cite: 8, 229, 238].

### 3. Visual Ergonomics & Interaction
*   **Scannability:** Use visual badges to categorize items, speeding up the selection process for users with short attention spans[cite: 227, 238].
*   **Mobile-First Design:** Hide secondary information inside collapsible accordion sections to reduce scroll fatigue on mobile screens, improving focused engagement[cite: 227, 237].
